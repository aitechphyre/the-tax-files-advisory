The Tax Files Advisory Tax Preparation Process

Tax rules are written in a language most of us were never taught. My process takes it one simple step at a time, so you can feel good about your tax situation.

Book a free call

Pick a time that works for you. We start with a free 15-minute consultation call, no pressure and no jargon, so I can hear what is going on in your life or business.

Gather your papers

Collect the basics: a photo ID, Social Security numbers (or ITIN letters) for your household, and any W-2s or 1099s you received. If you can, add receipts for things like donations, medical bills or business expenses, plus your bank details for a direct-deposit refund. Missing something? No problem, we will sort it out together. If you decide to move forward after your call, you will sign the engagement agreement and I will send you a secure activation email to set up your private client portal. Please upload your documents there, not by regular email. Check your spam folder if you do not see it.

Talk it through with me

We meet by phone or video, wherever you are in the U.S. I review the documents you uploaded to your portal for the current tax year ahead of time, then walk through them with you, so you know what it means and why it matters. We also talk about your goals and what you want from this tax year. This review covers the tax year we are preparing only. Reviewing prior-year returns is a separate service.

Get your taxes filed

From there, I prepare your return and look for every legitimate way to keep more of what you earn. You leave with clear, simple next steps, and you will know what to do now and what to plan for next year.
