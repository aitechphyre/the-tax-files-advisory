Client Portal

Your private, secure place to share tax documents with me. Please use the portal instead of email or text for anything sensitive.

Already set up? Log in

Use the secure link in your activation email to log in. Your email address is typically your username, and you chose your own password when you activated your account.

Lost your activation email or need help logging in? Email mum@taxfilesadvisory.com and I will help.

New to the portal?

Here is how to get your account set up. The portal is for clients who have signed an engagement agreement.

  1. 1

    Sign your engagement agreement

    After our free call, if you decide to move forward, we both sign the Client Engagement Agreement. Your portal is set up right after that.

  2. 2

    Look for your activation email

    I start your account and the portal sends an activation link to the email address I have on file for you. If you do not see it within a day, check your spam or junk folder, or let me know.

  3. 3

    Verify your information and create a password

    Click the activation link. It opens a secure setup page where you confirm your details and create your own password. Your email address is typically your username from then on.

  4. 4

    Log in and upload your documents

    Upload your tax documents for the current tax year. The checklist on the New Clients page shows what to gather. Missing something? Upload what you have and we will sort out the rest together.

Uploading your documents

Please do

  • Upload through your secure portal
  • Send PDFs or clear, well-lit photos of each page
  • Convert Excel spreadsheets to PDF first
  • Use clear file names, such as “W-2 Employer Name”
  • Upload documents for the current tax year only

Please do not

  • Email or text Social Security numbers, bank details, or tax documents
  • Post documents on social media
  • Share your portal password with anyone
  • Trust unexpected messages asking you to log in. If in doubt, email me first

I verify the identity of every new client before accepting documents. It is a simple step that protects you and your refund.
