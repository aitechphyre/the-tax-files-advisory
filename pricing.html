How pricing works

  • Fees are based on the complexity of your return and the number of forms required to file. Business packages are priced by annual gross business income, not net profit.
  • After our free call, I email your exact quote in writing, good for 72 hours. Work begins once you sign the Engagement Agreement and pay the deposit.
  • Applicable forms are included when required by your circumstances, including Form 8867 when your return claims certain credits or Head of Household filing status.
  • Each business package covers one business (one Schedule C). Each additional business is $150.
  • Additional fees may apply for bookkeeping, amended returns, prior-year returns or unusually complex transactions.
  • Fees do not include IRS or state filing penalties, interest or other government charges.
  • All deductions must meet IRS eligibility requirements. Estimated tax plans are based on the information you provide and are not a guarantee of future tax liability.
  • I never base my fee on the size of your refund.
