How I work

The Tax Files Advisory was built on a simple idea: tax preparation should be personal, clear, and based on trust. I’m Mum Monson, and I’m proud to run an independently owned business that delivers personalized, reliable tax solutions for individuals, families, and small businesses across Collin County and North Texas. I meet with clients by video, so I can help you from the comfort of your own home, anywhere in the U.S. And I treat every client like family.

From our first call, I focus on personalized service shaped around your situation. I take the time to get the details right, because accurate tax preparation and strong client relationships go hand in hand. My goal is a return you can rely on and a process that leaves you feeling confident.

My experience

I’ve spent the last six years working for a large tax preparation service company, handling complex, high-stakes tax scenarios for everyone from busy families to thriving small businesses. I have held an active IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN) for six straight years. While I am not a CPA or a Tax Attorney, I hold an MBA from Texas Woman’s University in Denton, Texas (2016) and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from The University of Texas at Dallas in Richardson, Texas (2004). Strict, year-round continuing education keeps me ahead of the constantly changing tax laws that impact your bottom line.

Before diving fully into my passion for tax consultation, my career crossed deep operational and financial landscapes. While working in the banking fintech sector, my role was to evaluate tax returns to verify a business’s true cash flow, financial stability, and ability to repay debt. I know firsthand that proper tax preparation ensures your internal financial statements align perfectly with your official tax filings—which is the most critical factor for commercial loan approval. If you want to fund your business or buy property, I know exactly what lenders look for.

Beyond banking, I worked with a prominent Dallas-based e-commerce fulfillment and technology provider, where I managed infrastructure for high-volume third-party logistics (3PL), multi-channel fulfillment, warehousing, and 24/7/365 customer support. Handling complex, high-SKU product catalogs for major direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands taught me exactly how inventory, overhead, and supply chain tech move in the real world. My professional background also spans intensive healthcare reconciliation—giving me a razor-sharp eye for tracking down missing dollars and keeping books perfectly balanced.

Why I started The Tax Files Advisory

Are you struggling to file your Schedule C, make sense of your profit and loss reports, or understand how paying self-employment taxes helps build your future Social Security benefits? You’re not alone—and you don’t have to figure it all out by yourself!

After years of trying to make sense of the complex U.S. tax system myself, I founded The Tax Files Advisory to help families and business owners turn tax-time stress into confident, proactive planning.

Filing taxes for a small business can be frustrating, but you don’t have to navigate it alone! Let’s schedule some time together, and I’ll help you walk through the forms, better understand your tax obligations, and explore potential tax credits you may qualify for but didn’t know existed.

Ready to feel more confident about your taxes? Click the “Email Me” button, and let’s start a conversation! I’d love to hear your questions and help you take the next step toward making smarter, more informed tax decisions.
